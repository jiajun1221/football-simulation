# Club logo sources

The following transparent PNG club badges were downloaded from
[FootyLogos](https://www.footylogos.com/) and retain their original club
trademarks:

- `west-bromwich-albion.png`
- `middlesbrough.png`
- `coventry-city.png`
- `blackburn-rovers.png`

They are used at low display sizes for club identification in the game UI.

The 20 promoted and new 2026-27 Champions League club badges were downloaded
as rendered Wikimedia/Wikipedia thumbnails by `download-2026-27-badges.mjs` on
2 September 2026. They remain trademarks of their respective clubs and are
used only for low-resolution club identification.
